Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This policy outlines the terms for cancellations by students and by Beyond Vectors.

Cancellation by Student

Enrollments cannot be cancelled once payment has been processed. All sales are final. If you are unable to attend the live sessions, you will retain lifetime access to all recordings and materials.

Deferral

If you are unable to attend the cohort you enrolled for, you may request to defer your enrollment to a future cohort, subject to availability. Deferral requests must be submitted at least 7 days before the cohort start date by emailing team@beyondvectors.com. Deferrals are granted at our discretion and are available once per enrollment.

Cancellation by Beyond Vectors

In the event that Beyond Vectors cancels a cohort entirely, enrolled students will receive a full refund or the option to transfer their enrollment to a future cohort. We will provide as much advance notice as possible in such cases.

Contact

For questions about your enrollment, contact us at team@beyondvectors.com.
